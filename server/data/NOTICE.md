# The Dictionary's word list

`dictionary.bin` (the Dictionary extra) is built by `scripts/build-dictionary.mjs` from two sources. Each is
changed only in form: entries are regrouped by word, shortened to their first two examples and eight similar words,
and pronunciations are rewritten from ARPAbet into IPA.

## Open English WordNet 2025

Meanings, examples, similar and opposite words, word forms, and some pronunciations.
<https://en-word.net>, <https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet>

## The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary

Pronunciations for words Open English WordNet has none for. <https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict>
